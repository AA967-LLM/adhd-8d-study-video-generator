⚡ The Dual-Speaker Bilateral Breakthrough

Monotone single-speaker monologues allow the ADHD brain's Default Mode Network to wander after 8 to 12 seconds. By alternating speakers across opposite ears, the brainstem must constantly re-center attention.

🔴 Alex (Left Ear — 85/15 Panned)
Marcus! In my mock exam, Question 26 asked whether digital services or products come first. Why did I get marked wrong for picking 'products based on services'?
🔵 Marcus (Right Ear — 15/85 Panned)
Because services are always built on top of products! The product is your core engine; the service is how value is delivered to the customer.
🔴 Alex (Left Ear — 85/15 Panned)
Got it! What about utility versus warranty? How do I lock down that pair?
🔵 Marcus (Right Ear — 15/85 Panned)
Utility is fit for PURPOSE (what it does). Warranty is fit for USE (availability, capacity, and security). Purpose versus performance!
